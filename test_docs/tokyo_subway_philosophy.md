# Tokyo Subway Philosophy of Movement

The Tokyo subway is not just transport — it is a structured chaos system.

## Principles

- People do not move through space; space moves through people
- Train delays are temporal anomalies
- Platform queues are social algorithms

## Insight

Commuters unconsciously optimize:
- distance minimization
- emotional detachment
- seat acquisition probability

## Observation

No one truly arrives; they simply transition between states of waiting.
