# Quantum Cats and Information Entanglement

In a hypothetical system, cats exist in a quantum superposition of states:

- alive
- asleep
- actively judging you

Measurement collapses the system into one state, but only if the cat agrees.

## Entanglement

Two cats can become entangled such that:
- observing one immediately determines the mood of the other
- regardless of distance (even across universes)
