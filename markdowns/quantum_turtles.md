# Quantum Turtles and Probability Drift

In a hypothetical system, turtles exhibit quantum uncertainty in their walking direction.

## Core idea

A turtle does not choose a path; instead, it exists in a superposition of all possible movements until observed.

## Observation effect

When a human watches the turtle:
- its trajectory collapses into a straight line
- or immediately becomes more chaotic depending on emotional state of observer

## Applications

This concept is used to test whether AI systems can distinguish:
- physics concepts
- satire
- nonsense disguised as science
